\nolinkurl{repro_runs/2026-08-11_fff_common_mode_gradient/}.
The package constructs the three atom-transition multigraphs and the
normalized and coarse opposite-potential edge systems on all $104645$
components of the $9777$ frozen tables.  It checks $2877258$ dual edges,

reports no error.  All $1080$ exceptional order-$6$ components are coarse;
exactly one observed order-$8$ component has positive sheet-only quotient

These finite uniqueness and $p_F=q_F$ observations are not universal claims,
and the $135$ order-$10$ tables remain diagnostic only.

\item[Paper sign and theorem audit]
\nolinkurl{repro_runs/2026-08-10_paper_symbol_sign_audit/}.
It exhaustively checks $1028307$ two-line trades on all reduced Latin squares
of orders $2$, $4$, and $6$, with zero sign-table failures.  It also supplies

this manuscript against its frozen result object.
\end{description}

Every package contains a README, exact commands, an environment record, input
and output manifests, and a package-level SHA-256 manifest.  Paths and elapsed
times are treated as volatile comparison fields; pattern counts, witness
sets, group-isotopy flags, and input hashes are not normalized away.

The computations use ordinary Python integer and permutation operations; no
random sampling enters Computational Theorems~\ref{cthm:small-orders}
or~\ref{cthm:order8}.  The repository also contains direct SAT/CP existence
investigations of order $10$, but those particular runs are deliberately
excluded from the theorem set because they end in timeouts rather than a
validated FFF model or a complete unsatisfiability certificate.  Order-$10$
nonexistence is established instead by the two complete cycle-palette master
searches in Computational Theorem~\ref{cthm:order10-nonexistence}.  The bounded
primitive-group clique computations are different again: their small finite
formulas are decided and independently proof-checked.

\subsection*{Data and code availability}
The historical collection retains the custom source programs, frozen small
inputs, solver commands, validation outputs, proof-check logs, and SHA-256
manifests used for the stated computational theorems.  The two large generated
order-$10$ graph files and their checkpoint trees are retained outside Git;
their hashes, sizes, builders, complete semantic graph audits, and path-configurable
rebuild commands are recorded in the corresponding repro packages.  The
external ANU and Wanless census files remain available from the cited data
pages; the exact frozen copies used here are identified by cryptographic
hashes and by the release audit above.  Solver binaries and third-party software environments are not included in the public export.  A
persistent DOI archive containing or durably linking the large external graph
artifacts has not yet been deposited and remains a pre-submission task.
