## Separate Compact Review

The external report covered exactly the former 44-page Compact and 89-page
Full files. It did not cover the new 12-page Compact. Its proof chain was
read separately:

- Latin two-line maps are fixed-point-free in all three views. Odd-order
  reasoning requires n>1; the group argument permits the trivial 2-group.
- Closure gives a finite regular permutation group; its regular action
  supplies the isotopy. The converse holds at every base row.
- Product permutations factor in all views, including equal-coordinate
  identity factors. The lcm proof includes both directions of pattern OR.
- Product-family closure is equivalent to closure of both factors using
  identity members, not cancellation of main classes.
- In two-element congruence fibres, every local binary Latin operation has
  XOR form. For an odd quotient cycle, the chosen row-bit difference toggles
  its voltage, proving the converse in every parastrophe.
- The explicit loop has an identity, unique row/column division, and the
  displayed associativity failure. A loop with closed left translations
  would be associative, so its group-isotopy failure follows.
- Computational statements remain separate: all-pattern inflation uses
  finite order-8 inputs, whereas the explicit nongroup family needs no census.
  The printed FFF12 table is fully scanned; order 10 is imported background.

Fresh Compact controls cover all 198 order-12 line pairs, the explicit loop,
products of orders 16/32 with closure failure, group and odd-order controls,
and all eight frozen patterns with C2 products. They do not freshly scan the
complete order-8 census. References close within this selected proof chain;
there is no hidden appeal to an omitted long-edition proof for its general
product/congruence theorems.
