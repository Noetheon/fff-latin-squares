\section{Reproducibility and evidence}\label{sec:reproducibility}

The source package separates general proofs from finite controls. Every
mathematical block reused in this edition is selected from the frozen
30 September source, with its source hash recorded by the edition builder.
The longer editions reuse those sources rather than maintaining competing
versions of a theorem. The editorial selection does not upgrade a claim.

\paragraph{Finite computational dependencies.}
The small-order enumeration is recorded in
\nolinkurl{repro_runs/2026-04-26_small_order_witness_reruns/}.
Its generator constructs the reduced tables rather than loading a cached
result, and checks Latinness, reduction, and uniqueness. The order-$8$
census is recorded in \nolinkurl{repro_runs/2026-04-26_order8_core/},
with a separate group-isotopy reconstruction in
\nolinkurl{repro_runs/2026-04-26_order8_closure_reconstruction/}.
The frozen compressed census input has SHA-256
\nolinkurl{e5980ff5bc4ac46cbbefd1f1333258d0905724c42a976c4afe61ea501a4ccf87}.
Matching this hash identifies the input; it does not independently prove
that the external census is complete.

\paragraph{Displayed examples and product controls.}
The order-$12$ table is the frozen \nolinkurl{data/order12_first.json}
from the non-power-of-two witness intake, SHA-256
\nolinkurl{8b71d6ecaa6ab86a806a0660529b404d8f3667db2b58b82bf5c72fed010fa416}.
The edition check compares the displayed table with that input and traverses
all $198$ line-pair permutations. It also constructs the order-$8$ loop
directly from its formula, checks its closure failure, tests products with
$C_2$ and $C_2^2$, and checks the eight frozen pattern controls.
These are small checks of the selected content, not fresh exhaustive
order-$8$ or order-$10$ computations. The general product results rest on
the proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:pattern-product} and the subsequent results,
not on these tests.

\paragraph{Rebuilding this edition.}
From the research repository root, the command
\begin{center}\small
\texttt{python3 -B tools/build\_paper\_editions.py}\\
\texttt{--output .audit/three-editions-check}
\end{center}
builds all three editions using the fixed evidence date, with shell escape
disabled. The output includes source-selection records, PDF hashes, page
counts, and compiler diagnostics. The companion
\nolinkurl{scripts/check_compact_examples.py} runs the finite checks above.
The source package is
\nolinkurl{manuscript/candidates/2026-09-30_three_editions/}.

The project is distributed for critical examination at
\url{https://noetheon.github.io/fff-latin-squares/}.
Repository checks establish specified file and computation properties,
not peer review, novelty, or a complete order spectrum.
