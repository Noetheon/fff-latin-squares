\subsection*{This edition's additional evidence}

This is a dated local working-copy snapshot, not a claim that the
public repository or a persistent archive already contains it.
Its source directory
\nolinkurl{manuscript/candidates/2026-09-27_research_snapshot/}
contains a theorem-to-evidence map, the captured proof notes and
small comparison objects, the build commands, and a dedicated audit.
The capture lists source hashes and the claim cutoff C251.
All inherited sources and earlier release PDFs are preserved.

The principal additions are the affine-family criterion and
classification (C165--C167), spectrum and row-fibred formulas
(C246--C247), binary quotient recovery and affine-orbit counting
(C248--C250), and the exact position--value channel (C245).
The order-$20$ source audit checks the complete $1024$-mask family,
not a sample. The higher-order class bounds are rigorous
consequences of that source evidence and C157, not physical scans
of all the outputs being counted. The two order-$160$ controls
are compared by exact sorted spectra, not only by hash equality.

The new finite audit records which controls were freshly rerun
and which earlier certified computations were only verified
against frozen reports. In particular, the expensive C157 master
searches and the scoped order-$18$ solver proofs are not silently
reported as fresh reruns of this paper build. Historical sign and
manuscript audit tools certify their own dated scope, not every
new theorem automatically.

SHA-256 provides byte identity, not correctness of mathematics,
source completeness, peer review, or literature priority.
The source archive's checksum manifest excludes itself and uses
a separate output manifest for the generated PDFs and reports.
The new PDFs are local review outputs; the two curated historical
PDF exceptions in Git are not replaced by this task.
