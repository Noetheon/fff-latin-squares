\section{Current search boundaries and research priorities}
\label{sec:current-boundaries}

Order $18$ remains undecided. A fixed input, permitted transformation,
and complete finite search space must accompany every local negative
result. The following developments are not a cumulative percentage of
an unrestricted search.

\begin{proposition}[Intercalates forced by paired blocks]
\label{prop:pairblock-intercalates}
If a normalized line family in any view of an order-$2m$ Latin square
preserves a partition into $m$ pairs, then its intercalate count is
$I=m^2+2K$ for some nonnegative integer $K$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Normalize a row view by its base row, identifying columns and symbols.
Every row has the form
$L(g,(x,t))=(\phi_g(x),t+f_g(x))$ on $[m]\times\mathbb F_2$.
Let $J$ flip every symbol pair. The two column maps of block $x$
satisfy $C_{x,1}=JC_{x,0}$. Thus their relative permutation is
conjugate to $J$ and has $m$ transpositions. The $m$ within-block
column pairs contribute exactly $m^2$ intercalates. Between two distinct
blocks, the relative maps for column bits $(0,0),(1,1)$ are identical,
as are those for $(0,1),(1,0)$. Their two-cycle contributions are
therefore even. Parastrophy gives the other views without changing
the intercalate count.
\end{proof}

At order $18$ a paired-block view therefore requires at least 81
intercalates and an odd total. This is a necessary condition, not
a proof that a hypothetical FFF18 square cannot have such a view.

\paragraph{Local distance certificates.}
One frozen FFT18 source has a certified same-label lower bound of
35 changed cells for even a symbol-F Latin target. A different frozen
FTF18 source has a bound of 37 cells for even a column-F target.
The supporting reductions use physical odd-cycle supports and Latin
replacement constraints; their retained UNSAT records have independently
checked DRAT traces. This paper update does not rerun those historical
proof checkers. Neither bound constructs a target at the stated distance
or excludes a more distant target. A small number of failing pairs
is not evidence of a small cell-edit distance to FFF.

\paragraph{Distinct normalizations with different coverage proofs.}
The earlier 14-case longest-zero-cycle normalization and alternative
33-case dual-positive normalization have now been refined to 13 and
31 cases, respectively, by Corollary~\ref{cor:nonnear-anchors}.
These are still alternatives: the extra column-sign constraint must not
be added to the 13 cases without another coverage proof. A further
three-positive-sign shortcut is false even on a frozen FFF8 control.
The paired baseline/overlay order-18 tests time out; they establish
neither a case exclusion nor a runtime improvement.

\paragraph{Other finite-family exclusions.}
The fixed-remainder contraction audit now covers the entire frozen
22-row-pair trade-mask family and the two quadratic controls, with
no FFF18 output. A separate theorem excludes FFF completions of principal
fixed-remainder two-point contractions of Steiner loops of order
$n\geq8$ divisible by four: their Latin completions are symmetric
of order $n-2\equiv2\pmod4$ and greater than two. Neither statement covers arbitrary
parents, nonprincipal Steiner contractions, or repairs changing
retained cells. The new unique-intercalate family has its different,
direct noncontraction obstruction in Corollary~\ref{cor:corner-noncontraction}.

Free-order-three incidence conditions and affine quotient no-goods
constrain specified symmetry subclasses only. The recent Mendelsohn,
four-transversal and repair pilots do not decide them. These model
derivations can be useful without being a breakthrough on unrestricted
existence. A shorter equivalent CNF is not, by itself, evidence of
a faster solver.

\paragraph{How further searches will be evaluated.}
Before extending a computation, specify its mathematical hypothesis,
covered space, baseline, resource limit and decisive outcome. Separate
general theorems, complete finite exclusions and heuristic observations.
Use positive and negative physical controls, compare genuinely different
methods, and require evidence of benefit before expanding a sequence
of similar timeout pilots. Literature comparison and external expert
review remain independent tasks. CPU utilization, file counts and
claim identifiers are not measures of distance to an order-$18$ solution.
