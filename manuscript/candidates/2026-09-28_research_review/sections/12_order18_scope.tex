\section{The precisely bounded order-18 frontier}
\label{sec:order18-scope}

None of the results in this section decides unrestricted order-$18$
FFF existence. They distinguish a lossless normalization from
exclusions of particular construction families and local contexts.

\begin{proposition}[A free-column same-sign normalization]
\label{prop:same-sign-normalization}
Every row-F Latin square of even order $n\geq4$ is isotopic to a
reduced square whose second row is a canonical even-cycle
permutation of positive sign, with zero in a longest cycle.
At order $18$ this gives a complete family of $14$ row-1 cases.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Among the row bijections $\rho_r:C\to S$, two, say $\rho_a,\rho_b$,
have the same sign. Their relative permutation
$\phi=\rho_a^{-1}\rho_b$ has positive sign, no fixed point,
and only even cycles. Choose a column $c_0$ on a longest cycle,
and a column labelling $\kappa$ taking its successive points to
$0,1,\ldots$. Label the other cycles on consecutive intervals
in decreasing order of length. Put
\[
 \sigma=\kappa\rho_a^{-1},\qquad
 \alpha(r)=\sigma(L(r,c_0)),\qquad
 L'(\alpha(r),\kappa(c))=\sigma(L(r,c)).
\]
All maps are bijections. The first row and column of $L'$ are
identity lines, $\alpha(a)=0$, $\alpha(b)=1$, and row 1 is
$\kappa\phi\kappa^{-1}$. This isotopy preserves all three pattern
bits. Conversely, a square in any retained case is an unrestricted
square. The even partitions of $18$ are twice the partitions of
$9$; exactly $14$ of the $30$ have an even number of parts and
therefore positive permutation sign.
\end{proof}

The first column is chosen freely before reduction. A model with
previously pinned cells or a distinguished column cannot use this
normalization without transporting those anchors. None of the
$14$ complete cases is excluded by the bounded model-size probes.

\paragraph{Completed construction exclusions.}
The following are exact scoped results in the frozen research
archive; they are not substitutions for the $14$ unrestricted cases.
One dependency needed for arbitrary transversals deserves an explicit
statement. It is not the standard-prolongation criterion of
Section~\ref{sec:affine-prime}.

\begin{proposition}[The cyclic-base dependency, C225]
\label{prop:cyclic17-transversal}
No standard one-point prolongation of the addition table of
$\mathbb F_{17}$ along any transversal is FFF.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Write a transversal as $(r,f(r),r+f(r))$, so both $f$ and $r+f(r)$
are permutations. For two old rows $r,s$, put
$t=(f(s)-f(r))/(r-s)$ in $\mathbb F_{17}$, using its representative
in $\{2,\ldots,16\}$. Cutting the two selected arrows in the original
$17$-cycle and inserting the new point gives cycles of lengths
$t$ and $18-t$. Thus row-F forces $t$ even for every pair.
All secant directions of the graph of $f$ lie among the eight
values $-\{2,4,\ldots,16\}$. The R\'edei--Megyesi direction theorem
requires at least ten directions for a noncollinear set of
$17$ points \cite[Theorem~1.1]{Somlai2022Directions}.
Consequently $f(r)=ar+b$ with $a\notin\{0,-1\}$.

Translations remove $b$. The old-row and old-column even-cycle
tests require the least positive residues of $-a$ and $-a^{-1}$
to be even; they leave only $a=1,5,7$. In the symbol view the
base operation is $r=s-c$. Replacing $c$ by $-c$ turns it into
addition and gives transversal coefficient $-a/(a+1)$.
The same old-line cycle test therefore requires the residue of
$a/(a+1)$ to be even. For $a=1,5,7$ those residues are respectively
$9,15,3$, all odd. Already these old-line pairs exclude FFF.
No claim about an arbitrary order-$18$ square is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{description}
\item[Translation-equivariant one-point family (C221).]
For complete maps $f:\mathbb F_{17}\to\mathbb F_{17}$ with
$f(0)=0$, the standard prolongation of $x+f(y-x)$ contains no
FFF square. A complete parastrophe/scaling reduction has three
nonaffine cases. The final case-2 conditional-lex CNF is UNSAT
with an independently checked DRAT trace, joining the previously
checked cases 3 and 4 and the finite affine exclusion.
Translation equivariance is an additional hypothesis.

\item[Arbitrary transversals of odd-map bases (C235).]
Among the $12513$ odd complete mappings, $12450$ bases fail a
necessary old-line-pair cycle screen, $15$ affine survivors reduce
to Proposition~\ref{prop:cyclic17-transversal}, and all $48$ nonaffine
survivors have individually checked UNSAT certificates for the
necessary transversal model. This covers every transversal for
these odd-map bases, not nonodd maps or arbitrary order-$17$ bases.
Indeed, for an affine complete map $f(t)=at$, relabelling rows
by $(1-a)x$ and columns by $ay$ turns $x+f(y-x)$ into addition.
These relabellings extend over the new point and transport every
transversal prolongation. The C225 proof and its finite controls
are retained explicitly in the companion snapshot.

\item[Fixed-remainder contraction of represented FFF20 classes (C251).]
For the $514$ represented classes of
Computational Theorem~\ref{cthm:fff20-514}, delete an order-two
Latin subsquare and refill only holes left by its deleted symbols,
keeping all other surviving triples fixed. This produces no FFF18
square. The finite screen covers all $1024$ trade masks and the two
quadratic controls. All $9728$ base completions are TTT; proper-mask
completion spaces coincide with a base case, and the quadratic
controls have no Latin completion. The rule is invariant under
isotopy and parastrophy because it is stated on the three sorts
and their retained triples. It excludes neither other FFF20
classes nor repairs that change retained entries.
\end{description}

\paragraph{A genuinely three-view local conflict.}
One frozen reduced ten-row prefix in case 14 has the five further
conditions
\[
 L(11,6)=2,\quad L(11,9)=7,\quad L(11,10)=6,\quad
 L(11,11)=13,\quad L(11,16)=3,
\]
with zero-based indexing. At the thirteen-row stage, each of the
seven proper subsets of the three view constraints has a separately
validated partial Latin solution with no closed odd cycle in its
active views. All three views together are UNSAT with a verified
DRAT trace. The seven solutions need not be the same table. This
is an exact joint obstruction for that prefix and those five cells,
not a whole-case exclusion or a universal claim that two views
never suffice.

The full row-fibred and affine-orbit constructions do not resolve
these open searches. In particular, the explicit FFF98 control
already rules out any universal obstruction based solely on
$n\equiv2\pmod8$. More computation on a selected source is not
coverage of all Latin squares; an eventual negative decision must
close an exhaustive, justified search space.
