# Review and Correction Requests

Please keep feedback specific and evidence-backed. Useful contributions include:

- A counterexample to a named proposition, with an explicit Latin table.
- A missing hypothesis or an unjustified step, with section/theorem references.
- A reproducibility failure, with the command, environment and minimal output.
- A prior result or classification that changes the novelty or dependency picture.
- A finite coverage or parser failure supported by an executable small example.

Do not upload personal correspondence, credentials, identifiable local paths or
unrelated documents. Use Issues for concrete defects and Discussions for broader
questions. Clearly disclose automated assistance and distinguish conjectures from
verified computations. No expert review, endorsement or coauthorship is inferred
from a comment. Code reuse/licensing remains subject to `RIGHTS.md`.

The maintainer supplies the publication infrastructure, not claimed mathematical
expertise. Answers may require external subject-matter examination.
