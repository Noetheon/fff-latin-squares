# Selected E9 Extension: Release Review

This release candidate adds only the complete two-free-coordinate E9 theorem
to the C280 baseline, not all later working results. The papers have 12 / 46 /
91 total pages. Unrestricted order 18 remains open; C38 remains disproved.
No external peer review, literature priority, journal acceptance, DOI or new
authorship/licensing decision is asserted.

The proof and its limitations are in the
[candidate review](../manuscript/candidates/2026-10-02_e9_symmetry_review/REVIEW_AND_LIMITATIONS.md).
Source originals and earlier receipts remain frozen. Only the three explicitly
named current PDF aliases are replaced, with predecessor/new hashes in
`PUBLIC_SNAPSHOT_2026-10-02.json`. The public byte manifest binds all shipped files.

## Privacy and Delivery

The candidate was held on 2 October because the mandatory local privacy guard
could not complete OCR on a new cover image. Incomplete inspection was treated
as a failure, not a pass; no commit or push occurred during that attempt.

On 3 October, the unchanged guard successfully checked all three current cover
images and PDFs. All seven selected sources and 54 source-package payloads still
matched their frozen hashes, as did all 37 saved candidate files. The hold was
resolved without changing scientific evidence, bypassing hooks, or replacing
the inspection mechanism. The edition's scientific cutoff remains unchanged.

Local checks do not by themselves establish delivery. Publication additionally
requires inspection of the exact staged bytes and pseudonymous commit identity,
the active privacy hooks, a concurrent-update check, and an ordinary push. The
commit's required CI and Pages deployment must pass, and the served PDFs must
match the three digests in `PUBLIC_SNAPSHOT_2026-10-02.json`. GitHub records the
actual commit and workflow outcomes separately from this prepublication review.

## Limits

The historical order-10 exhaustive searches and large UNSAT certificates were
not rerun; their existing evidence boundaries remain explicit. No new solver
campaign, literature-priority certification or external human review was done.
The new finite controls support the written conditional proof, not unrestricted
order-18 nonexistence or a new Latin18 construction.
