What does FFF mean?

Match equal symbols between two rows and follow the resulting permutation of columns. An odd cycle would make the row view T; its absence for every row pair makes it F.

The column and symbol views have their own induced permutations. FFF requires all three views to pass. One highlighted pair is only an illustration, not the full check.
